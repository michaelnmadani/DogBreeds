Showing allergy-friendlier breeds only. No cat is truly hypoallergenic: the main allergen, Fel d 1, comes from saliva and skin rather than fur, and levels vary more between individual cats than between breeds. Before you commit:
  • Spend time with the specific cat, ideally more than once.
  • Females and neutered males produce less allergen than intact males.
  • Cat food with an anti-Fel d 1 antibody cut active allergen on cats' hair by 47% on average in one study.
  • Keep the cat out of the bedroom, use HEPA filters, wash bedding weekly and let someone else clean the litter box.
  • If allergies are severe or trigger asthma, talk to an allergist first.
